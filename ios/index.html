Download for Mac 0.6 MB · macOS 14+
What it reads
The Apple Books and Kindle databases already on your Mac. Nothing else, and only when you point it at them.
What leaves your Mac
Title and author, sent to Open Library to look up page counts. That is the only server the app ever contacts.
Where your library goes
Your own iCloud, which is how your iPhone picks it up. There is no WoodWork account, no server of ours, and no analytics.
